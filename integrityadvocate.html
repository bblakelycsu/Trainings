Getting Started

!

Do Not Create Fake Tests for Student Practice

Do not schedule a fake quiz or test to verify that students can access Integrity Advocate (IA). Each monitored session uses a license. Instead, direct students to the official IA Practice Session (opens in new tab) so they can test their camera, microphone, and browser settings without consuming licenses.

Every proctored session is reviewed by a live person, not just AI. Results are typically available within 24 hours after the student completes their session. You will see the status update in the IA widget under the Participants tab once the review is complete.

The IA widget is automatically added to the bottom of all course homepages. You do not need to install or add it yourself. Use the widget to enable monitoring on activities, review results, and manage sessions.

Enable IA on an Activity

Finding the IA Widget

Scroll to the bottom of your course home page to find the section labeled Integrity Advocate - Secure Proctoring Platform. This is the IA widget where you will enable monitoring, review results, and manage sessions.

Step-by-Step: Enabling Monitoring on a Quiz

Important: You must create your quiz in Brightspace first. IA can only be enabled on activities that already exist.

  1. In the IA widget, click the Activities tab. A table of all quizzes, assignments, and content activities in your course will appear.
  2. Find the quiz you want to monitor. Each row in the table has three options:
    • Enable Integrity Advocate (checkbox) — Check this box to turn on IA proctoring for this activity.
    • Use Grade Item (checkbox) — Check this box to create an IA Status column in the Gradebook that automatically updates with each student's proctoring result (Valid or Invalid).
    • Rules Selection — Choose either Demo (for testing purposes only) or Level 4 (CSU's standard proctoring level with all rules enabled).
  3. Select Level 4 as the rules level, then click Select Rules.
  4. Review the rules that will apply (see below), then click Submit. Students will be informed of these rules when they begin the quiz.

If you do not have more than one activation package, simply click Select Rules to proceed with the default configuration.

Level 4 Proctoring Rules

Use Grade Item

When you check Use Grade Item next to an activity, IA creates an IA Status column in your Brightspace Gradebook. After a session is reviewed, this column automatically updates to show whether each student's session was Valid or Invalid. This gives you a quick at-a-glance view of proctoring results alongside your other grade columns.

If a status is later overridden in the IA widget, you will need to click Update Grade in the Participants tab to sync the change to the Gradebook.

Reviewing Proctored Results

How to Review Results

Every proctored session is reviewed by a live person. Results are typically available within 24 hours after the student completes the session.

From within the IA widget at the bottom of your course home page, click the Participants tab to see all session data for a specific activity.

  1. Select the exam you want to review from the activity list.
  2. Use the Select Status dropdown to filter by status (e.g., Invalid (Rules) or Invalid (ID)) to quickly find sessions that need attention.

The Participants Tab

The Participants tab is your primary view for reviewing student proctoring sessions. After selecting an activity, the tab displays a table with the following columns:

ColumnWhat It Shows
ParticipantThe student's name. Click the name to open their full session details, including captured images and reviewer notes.
StatusThe current session status: In Progress, Valid, Invalid (ID), or Invalid (Rules). See the status descriptions below.
Start DateThe date and time the student began the proctored session.
End DateThe date and time the student completed the session. Displays "Incomplete" if the student is still taking the exam or left without finishing.
OverrideAn action link that allows you to change the session status (e.g., from Invalid to Valid). See "Overriding a Status" below.
Update GradeIf Use Grade Item was enabled for this activity, click this button to sync the session status to the Brightspace Gradebook.

Understanding Session Statuses

There are four possible statuses for each participant session:

In Progress

If an end date and time are shown, the session is being reviewed by a live reviewer. If the end date shows "Incomplete," the student is currently taking the quiz or started the session but left without completing it.

Valid

The session is complete with no violations. A live reviewer has confirmed the session was clean.

Invalid (ID)

The student submitted an unreadable or non-approved ID and needs to resubmit. IA emails the student directly with a resubmission link. You can also find this link under the participant's Status in the Participants tab.

Invalid (Rules)

A live reviewer has confirmed a violation of the selected proctoring rules for the quiz.

Reviewing a Learner Attempt

For Invalid statuses, click the participant's name in the Participants tab to access the session details, including images captured during the quiz and reviewer notes indicating what was flagged.

Note: You can only view session recordings and data for students enrolled in your own course. You do not have access to sessions from other instructors' courses.

When to Override a Status

An override changes a student's proctoring result after a live reviewer has already made a determination. Common reasons to override include:

  • False flag: The reviewer marked the session Invalid, but after watching the recording you determine the student did not actually violate a rule (e.g., briefly glancing away was flagged as disengagement, or a family member walked through the background momentarily).
  • Approved accommodation: The student has a documented accommodation (e.g., Disability Access Center approval) that permits a condition the rules would otherwise flag, such as the presence of an aide or use of assistive technology.
  • Technical issue: A connectivity drop or camera glitch caused an Invalid result, and you can confirm the student was not at fault.
  • ID resubmission accepted: A student received Invalid (ID) but has since provided acceptable identification, and you want to update their status to Valid.

Use your professional judgment. If you are unsure whether an override is appropriate, contact partners@integrityadvocate.com for guidance.

How to Override a Status

You can override a Valid, Invalid (ID), or Invalid (Rules) status from the Participants tab:

  1. Find the participant in the Participants tab, then click Override in their row.
  2. Click the dropdown under Status Override, select the new status, and enter a reason for the change. Click Submit.
  3. Click OK when prompted to confirm.
  4. To view the override history, click Override again to see the reason and who made the change.

Important: If Use Grade Item is enabled for this activity, click the Update Grade button in the participant's row after overriding. This syncs the updated status to the IA Status column in your Brightspace Gradebook.

Exporting Results

You can export participant session data for record-keeping or reporting. From the Participants tab, click the Export button to download a file containing participant names, statuses, start and end dates, and any override information for the selected activity. This is useful for maintaining records of proctored exam results or sharing data with your department.

How to Exclude Learners from IA

When a bypass code is entered during the Integrity Advocate (IA) pre-check process, the learner will be allowed to proceed without IA running during the test session.

  1. Once the learner is prompted for the IA pre-check, click the gear icon located in the upper right corner.
  2. Manually enter the bypass code for the learner, then click the green checkmark to continue. The learner may begin the exam.

To request or delete bypass codes, contact partners@integrityadvocate.com.

Proctor Pro CSU's Proctoring Activation Level

Chicago State University uses Proctor Pro, Integrity Advocate's (IA) highest-stakes proctoring tier. This includes every available proctoring rule and protection option, designed for high-stakes assessments like exams and quizzes.

A lighter-touch tier (Proctor Light) is available for lower-stakes assessments. All settings below are managed by CTRE/OOIT. If you have questions about the configuration, email elearning@csu.edu.

Proctoring Rules & Discretion Levels

Each rule can be set to one of two modes:

Harsh The student is immediately blocked from continuing the moment the behavior is detected. They must resolve the issue or start a new attempt.

Discretion A live human reviewer applies professional judgment. The behavior is noted, but the student is only flagged if the reviewer determines it was sustained or intentional (e.g., a brief glance away vs. reading from notes).

RuleLevelModeWhat This Means for Students
Not engaged2 Discretion The student appears disengaged from the exam (e.g., looking away from the screen for extended periods). A brief lapse won't be flagged; the reviewer looks for sustained disengagement.
Out of view2 Discretion The student leaves the camera's view. A single brief moment (e.g., reaching for something) won't be flagged, but repeated or extended absence from the frame will.
Collaborative applications3 Harsh Any use of a collaborative app (Zoom, Slack, Discord, Teams, etc.) immediately blocks the student from continuing. They must close the app and start a new attempt.
Communicated with others3 Discretion The student appears to be talking to someone during the exam. A brief interruption (e.g., a child entering the room) won't be flagged; a sustained conversation will.
Multiple participants3 Discretion More than one person is visible on camera. Someone briefly passing through the background won't be flagged; someone sitting nearby for an extended period will.
Possible use of remote access application3 Harsh A remote access tool (TeamViewer, AnyDesk, etc.) is detected on the student's computer. This immediately blocks the student from continuing.
Smart device was used3 Discretion A phone, tablet, or smartwatch is detected in the camera view. A brief glance (e.g., silencing a notification) won't be flagged; actively using the device will.
Headphones / earbuds were used4 Discretion The student is wearing headphones or earbuds. Momentary use (putting them on and quickly removing them) won't be flagged; wearing them throughout the session will.
Possible use of external resources4 Harsh AI detects patterns suggesting the student is referencing materials off-screen (e.g., repeated off-screen eye movement). This immediately blocks the student. You can use Video Feed Out to review the recording yourself and decide whether to override the flag.
Room and desk scan inadequate4 Discretion The student's room or desk scan did not adequately show the testing environment. The reviewer applies judgment (e.g., a family photo on the desk vs. a phone partially hidden behind a book).

Browser Recommendation

Google Chrome is highly recommended for all proctored exams. Screen monitoring and full-screen enforcement only work in Chrome and Chromium-based browsers (such as Edge). Safari and Firefox do not support all IA monitoring features, which may cause issues during your session.

Make sure to disable any AI browser extensions (ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.) before starting your exam. If an AI extension is detected, you will be blocked from proceeding until it is removed or disabled.

How can I make sure my camera is working before starting my activity?

If your camera is not loading, follow these troubleshooting steps in order:

  1. Free Up Your Camera — Close any other applications that might be using your camera (e.g., Zoom, Teams, FaceTime) and close other browser tabs.
  2. Check & Reset Browser Permissions — If you accidentally blocked the camera previously:
    • Click the Lock icon in the address bar next to the website URL.
    • Locate the Camera setting and ensure it is set to "Allow" or click "Reset Permissions."
    • Refresh the page to apply the changes.
  3. Still having trouble?
    • Clear Cache: Clear your browser cache or perform a hard refresh, then try again.
    • Check Device Settings: Ensure your operating system has allowed the browser to access the camera in your privacy settings.
    • Try a Different Device: Work-managed computers often have strict firewalls. If possible, switch to a personal computer.

Violation Emails & ID Privacy

What should I do if I received an email about a potential violation?

If you received an email about a potential violation, it means there may have been activity during your session that didn't follow the rules set for your exam. Here's what to do:

  1. Open the email and carefully read the details provided.
  2. If you have questions or believe there's been a mistake, contact your instructor directly. They are the only ones who can decide what happens next.

Note: Integrity Advocate (IA) only provides the proctoring service. Any decisions or actions regarding violations must come from your instructor or institution.

Can I hide sensitive details on my ID and only show my face and name?

Yes. When you are asked to take a photo of your ID, you can block out sensitive information before submitting it. After taking the photo, look for the Edit button. Then:

  1. Click and drag over the area you want to block (like your ID number or address).
  2. Release your mouse or finger — the selected area will be covered.
  3. Once you are satisfied, submit the edited photo.

This allows you to show only what is needed: your name and face.
