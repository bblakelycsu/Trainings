How can I make sure my camera is working before starting my activity?

If your camera is not loading, follow these troubleshooting steps in order:

  1. Free Up Your Camera — Close any other applications that might be using your camera (e.g., Zoom, Teams, FaceTime) and close other browser tabs.
  2. Check & Reset Browser Permissions — If you accidentally blocked the camera previously, you will need to reset your permissions:
    • Click the Lock icon in the address bar next to the website URL.
    • Locate the Camera setting and ensure it is set to "Allow" or click "Reset Permissions."
    • Refresh the page to apply the changes.
  3. Still having trouble?
    • Clear Cache: Clear your browser cache or perform a hard refresh, then try again.
    • Check Device Settings: Ensure your operating system (Windows/macOS) has allowed the browser to access the camera in your privacy settings.
    • Try a Different Device: Work-managed computers often have strict firewalls. If possible, switch to a personal computer.

What devices and browsers can I use with Integrity Advocate?

Integrity Advocate works on most modern computers, phones, and tablets as long as your device has a working camera, microphone, and stable internet. However, certain monitoring features (like full-screen monitoring or screen capture) are only supported on desktop and laptop browsers.

Can I hide sensitive details on my ID and only show my face and name?

Yes. When you are asked to take a photo of your ID, you can block out sensitive information before submitting it. After taking the photo, look for the Edit button. Then:

  1. Click and drag over the area you want to block (like your ID number or address).
  2. Release your mouse or finger — the selected area will be covered.
  3. Once you are satisfied, submit the edited photo.

This allows you to show only what is needed: your name and face.
