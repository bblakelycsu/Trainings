Get in

Two ways to open your syllabus

Simple Syllabus is only for the CSU campus community. You sign in with your CSU Single Sign-On (SSO).

Inside your course

The My Syllabus tab

Brightspace course→My Materials→My Syllabus

Go to brightspace.csu.edu (opens in new tab), open your course, and the syllabus for that class opens right there.

All your classes at once

The Simple Syllabus link

chicagostate.simplesyllabus.com→CSU SSO

Log in at chicagostate.simplesyllabus.com (opens in new tab) to see your Student Dashboard. Bookmark it so it's always one tap away.

See "Sit tight"? Your instructor hasn't published the syllabus yet. Check back, or ask your instructor. See "Refused to connect"? Jump to Fix it.
Cross-listed course? If My Syllabus shows an error page with a list of links, just choose the link for your section.
The CSU rule
80%

Read it. Then acknowledge it.

At CSU, you must read at least 80% of each syllabus and then acknowledge it. Simple Syllabus tracks how far you've gone through the syllabus, measured toward the last section.

Your instructor can see how many times you viewed it, how much you read, and whether you acknowledged it. Best habit: read the whole thing in week one, and come back whenever you have a question about due dates, grading, or policies.

What you can do

More than a PDF

Your Student Dashboard has tools to keep every class organized.

My Courses

All the syllabi for the classes you're enrolled in, organized by term.

  1. Click My Courses in the left navigation.
  2. Find your class with the Search and filter tool.
  3. Click the thumbnail or View for the full page, or Print for a PDF.
Need an old syllabus? Use the term drop-down or filter by All courses. Hover over a syllabus to view or download it.

Follow for updates

Instructors can update a syllabus during the term. Click Follow to be notified when it changes. When automatic notifications turn on at launch, you'll be notified of updates for your active classes.

Material List

Click the Material List icon for one shopping list of the required textbooks from all your classes. Books your instructors add are linked to Follett.

Syllabus Library: look before you register

Search class syllabi campus-wide. Click Syllabus Library in the left navigation and filter by keyword, term, organization, subject, course number, or instructor, or browse by college and department.

Planning next term? Look up last term's syllabus for a class. To see every section, filter by Term, Subject, and Course number only. Some sections may be Private and visible only to enrolled students.

View or print

View opens a full-page version that works on your phone. Print downloads a PDF to save or print.

Fix it

Quick fixes for common problems

Try these first. Most problems come from browser settings.

I see "Refused to connect" or "Sit tight"+

"Sit tight" usually means your instructor hasn't published the syllabus yet.

For either message, make sure third-party cookies are allowed (see below). Simple Syllabus uses a cookieless sign-in, but some browser settings can still block it. Then try a different browser, such as Firefox.

Still stuck? Email help@simplesyllabus.com. If the error has a More Details button, click it and include a screenshot of the details.

Using a phone or tablet+
  • iPhone and iPad: in Settings, turn on "Allow Cross-Website Tracking."
  • Safari: turn off "Prevent Cross-Site Tracking." It's on by default and stops the syllabus from opening inside Brightspace.
  • Pulse, Chrome, and other apps: turn on "Allow Cross-Site Tracking." It's off by default.
How to allow third-party cookies+

Google Chrome: click More (three dots) → Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → Allow third-party cookies.

Mozilla Firefox: go to Brightspace, click the shield icon left of the address bar, and turn off Enhanced Tracking Protection for the site. The page reloads and allows the connection on that site only.

Safari: open Safari settings (Preferences on older versions) → Privacy → uncheck Prevent cross-site tracking.

Microsoft Edge: click the three dots → Settings → open the cookies settings → turn off Block third-party cookies.

I don't see the latest version+

Do a hard refresh to clear the saved copy of the page and load the newest version:

  • Chrome or Firefox on Mac: Command + Shift + R
  • Chrome or Firefox on Windows: Ctrl + Shift + R, or Ctrl + F5
  • Safari: Command + Option + R, or hold Shift and click Reload
  • Edge: Ctrl + F5, or hold Ctrl and click Refresh

Clear the cache in Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data → choose what to clear → Clear.

It still won't load in Chrome+
  • Try a Chrome Incognito window. If it works there, an extension may be blocking the connection. Extensions that block pop-ups or plugins can interfere. Turn extensions off, then back on one at a time to find the culprit.
  • Using more than one Google account (personal and school)? Sign out of all accounts except the one you use for your courses.
Which browsers work?+

All major browsers: Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge.
