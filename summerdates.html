Summer PTM Course Evaluation Date Calculator Summer 2026

Enter the course end date (Saturday) for each PTM group below to generate survey windows, instructor email reminders, and report access dates. All summer courses end on a Saturday. There are no finals in summer term.

1. Enter Course End Dates
Summer Term Rules: All summer courses end on a Saturday. The survey closes the day before the course ends (Friday at 11:59 PM) and opens 6 days before the close (Sunday at 11:59 PM), giving students 5 effective days (Monday–Friday) to complete evaluations. There are no finals in summer; no final-week buffer is needed.

Future note: Starting Summer 2027, OLHY and UNIV evaluations will run in the same project.
Rules used for each PTM:
• Survey Close: 1 day before course end — Friday at 11:59 PM
• Survey Open (System): 6 days before Survey Close — Sunday at 11:59 PM
• Survey Open (Effective): 1 day after Survey Open system date — Monday (first full day students see the survey)
• Survey Window: 5 effective days (Monday through Friday)
• Instructor Emails: 3 weeks, 2 weeks, and 1 week before the Survey Open date (sent at 8:00 AM)
• Admin Report Access (System): 8 days after course end (11:59 PM); effective first full day = next day
• Instructor Report Access: 15 days after course end (11:59 PM); effective first full day = next day
• DDS: Dynamic Date Session — dates are course-specific and may vary by section.
