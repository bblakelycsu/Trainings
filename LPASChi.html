About LPAS

Built on a Foundation of Trust & Legacy

LPAS — Legacy Preservation and Asset Stabilization — was founded on a simple belief: every homeowner deserves a partner who treats their property with the same care and urgency they would their own.

We specialize in helping property owners protect, stabilize, and strengthen their most valuable assets. Whether you're facing an urgent need or planning proactively, our team brings deep expertise and a personal touch to every engagement.

Based in Chicago, we understand the unique challenges that local homeowners face — and we're here to help you navigate them with confidence.

Genuine Care
