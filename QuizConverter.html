📝 Question Formatting Guide (Read Before Pasting!) ▼

Key Formatting Rules

  • Each question goes on its own line, followed by answer lines on subsequent lines.
  • For Multiple Choice and Multi-Select, mark correct answers with an asterisk (*) at the very beginning of the answer line.
  • For True/False, simply type True or False on the line after the question. Do NOT use an asterisk for True/False questions.
  • For Short Answer (SA), type SA before your question text.
  • For Matching, type match before your question and separate pairs with / (space-slash-space).
  • For Written Response, write only the question with no answer lines, then leave a blank line.
  • Separate each question block with a blank line (especially WR and SA types).
  • Question numbers (like 1. or 3.) are optional and will be stripped automatically.

Points, Hints & Feedback (Optional)

  • To set point value, add points: 5 on its own line after your answer lines. Default is 1 point if not specified.
  • To add a hint, add hint: Your hint text here on its own line after your answer lines.
  • To add feedback, add feedback: Your feedback text here on its own line after your answer lines.
  • These optional lines must come after all answer lines and before the blank line that separates questions.
  • You can use any combination: just points, just a hint, just feedback, or all three together.
  • Order does not matter: points:, hint:, and feedback: can appear in any order.
T/F True/False
Question on one line. Answer (True or False) on the next line. No asterisk needed. Optionally add points:, hint:, and feedback:.
Water consists of hydrogen and oxygen.
True
points: 2
hint: Think about the chemical formula H2O
feedback: Correct! Water is H2O.
MC Multiple Choice
Question on one line. Each answer on a new line. Put * before the one correct answer. Optionally add points:, hint:, feedback:.
Which of the following is a planet?
Sun
*Earth
Proxima Centauri
points: 5
feedback: Earth is the only planet listed here.
MS Multi-Select
Same as MC, but put * before all correct answers (2 or more).
Which of the following are planets?
Sun
*Earth
*Mars
Proxima Centauri
WR Written Response
Question on one line. No answer lines. Leave a blank line after.
Tell me about yourself.
SA Short Answer
Type SA before your question text. No answer lines. Leave a blank line after.
SA What is your favorite food?
MAT Matching
Type match before question. Each answer: choice / match with spaces around the slash.
match Match the number with its spelling.
3 / three
1 / one
12 / twelve
