Create a personalized self-care plan for your HCE certification journey. Intentionally identify and implement self-care practices that align with your educational pedagogy and CARMA.

How to Complete This Form
Review the eight types of self-care below. Each card includes a definition and examples to guide your thinking. Use the text areas to write your own self-care practices for any categories that resonate with you. You do not need to fill out every section. Focus on the areas that feel most meaningful to your personal journey.
Privacy notice: Your responses are not saved or stored anywhere. Once you close or refresh this page, everything you have written will be permanently deleted. To keep a copy of your self-care plan, use the Print / Save as PDF button at the bottom of the page before leaving.
CARMA Principles
The foundation for rooting your self-care in culture, empowerment, connection, purpose, and possibility.

Culture

Awareness of one's own and others' racial and social identities

Agency

The ability to act, create, and change root causes of personal and community challenges

Relationship

The capacity to create, sustain, and grow healthy connections with others

Meaning

The discovery of who we are, where we are going, and what purpose we serve

Aspirations

Exploring possibilities and accomplishing goals for personal and collective wellbeing
