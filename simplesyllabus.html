1. Get Started: Access Your Syllabus

  • From Brightspace: Go to brightspace.csu.edu (opens in new tab), open your course, select My Materials, then click My Syllabus.
  • From Simple Syllabus directly: Go to chicagostate.simplesyllabus.com (opens in new tab) and log in with your CSU Single Sign-On (SSO). Make sure third-party cookies are not blocked in your browser.

Your dashboard sorts your syllabi into two columns: To Do (syllabi to complete and submit) and Completed. Each item shows the course or section title, term, course number, status, and assigned editors. Click Edit to open the syllabus.

Your due date is your course start date. It varies by course length (16-week, 12-week, 8-week, and so on). Students can open Spring 2027 syllabi starting December 12, 2026.

First time or repeat? Most faculty (about 90%) are creating their first syllabus in Simple Syllabus this term, so you will build it once. For courses offered again after Fall 2026, your most recent syllabus for that course copies forward automatically to the next term offering.

Banner Updates and Instructors of Record

  • Course details, instructor assignments, and enrollments come from Banner. Banner updates Simple Syllabus and Brightspace twice a day, at 4:00 AM and 4:00 PM. Once a change is approved, wait for the next update for it to appear.
  • Only instructors of record appear on the syllabus. If an instructor who should be listed is missing, or a course is missing from or should be removed from your dashboard, your department chair completes the Instructor Authorization Form in Adobe Sign (select Start from library, open the workflow, and choose Instructor Authorization Form). The registrar then updates Banner. An instructor missing from the record also will not have access to the course in Brightspace.
  • Do not add the instructor manually in Simple Syllabus. When Banner adds them, the manual entry becomes a duplicate on the syllabus.
  • If course information (title, credit hours, meeting time, catalog description) is wrong, email the registrar at registrar@csu.edu to correct it in Banner. It cannot be edited in Simple Syllabus.

2. CSU Settings at a Glance

  • Who can see it: Campus Community. Only the CSU campus community can view syllabi, through single sign-on. This setting is fixed; instructors cannot change it.
  • Due date: Your course start date. It varies for 16-, 12-, and 8-week courses.
  • Students: In Brightspace, open the course, select My Materials, then My Syllabus. Spring 2027 syllabi open to students on December 12, 2026. Students can bookmark chicagostate.simplesyllabus.com (opens in new tab).
  • Auto-import: On. Your most recent syllabus for the same course fills in automatically.
  • Import, Add, and Reorder: On.
  • Approvals: Not enabled. When you click Submit, your syllabus publishes.
  • Accessibility check: Required before you can submit.
  • Attach documents: Off. Email elearning@csu.edu if you need it (see examples below).
  • Brightspace calendar sync: Not turned on. Build your schedule in the Calendar/Schedule component.
  • Reminder emails: If you have not completed your syllabus, Simple Syllabus emails you 14, 7, and 3 days before the due date; on the due date; and 3, 7, and 14 days after.
  • Editing window: You can edit and resubmit through the last day of the term (the Banner term end date). After that, the syllabus is archived and locked.
  • Reset to template: Email elearning@csu.edu to have a component reset to template content.
  • Coming at launch, Auto-follow: All students enrolled in an active section will be notified of updates to a syllabus.

3. Edit Your Components

Your syllabus is divided into components from the CSU template. Components you cannot edit are still visible to you; everything editable is ready for your content. Help text for each component appears on the right side.

  • Instructor components: Click the pencil icon next to your information. Your name and email populate from Banner; add details such as office hours, then save. Do not use Add new instructor for missing instructors (see Banner Updates above).
  • Content components: Type directly into the text area. Use the toolbar to format text and add links, images, videos, and tables. Add website links in components labeled HTML so they convert to clickable links. The block icon in the toolbar inserts auto-populated data points into the component; it is not the same as adding a new component.
  • Objectives components: Click the pencil icon next to Add new objective, complete the required fields, and click Save. Repeat for each objective. Drag and drop to reorder. To edit one, click its pencil icon; to remove it, click Delete.
  • Materials components: Click the pencil icon, complete the fields, and click Save. Repeat for each material. Follett is the default: when you add a book, it is linked to Follett for your course. You can add other materials too, including with the Google book search. Drag and drop to reorder.
  • Schedule components: Click + Add new item to add a row, then click into a cell to type. Highlighted cells are required. Column headers come from the template.

Formatting tips: For best results, type new content rather than pasting. Use the built-in editor to set font, size, color, style, and spacing. If you copy from Word, paste into Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit in plain text mode (Mac) first, then copy from there. Build tables with the table tool; do not insert a screenshot of a table.

Good to know: When you edit a component that has template content, your edits replace that content in your syllabus, and it no longer updates from the template. The Reset button returns a component to its template content; resetting removes your edits in that component only. Need help resetting? Email elearning@csu.edu.

Locked Sections

Some components are locked and cannot be edited by instructors. Course details such as the course title, CRN, credit hours, modality, meeting time, instructor of record, requisites, and catalog description populate from Banner. University statements, such as the Statement from Abilities Office, Emergency Evacuation, and Plagiarism/Academic Misconduct, are managed by the university so every syllabus has consistent, approved language.

If you notice an error in a university statement, email elearning@csu.edu so it can be corrected at the template level. Errors in Banner data are corrected by the registrar: email registrar@csu.edu.

4. Required Components (Orange Outline)

Components with an orange border and a "Required" label at the bottom must be completed before you can submit. For example, Course Requirements and/or Course Policies is required: add your attendance policy, class participation, exams, quizzes, papers, technology assignments, clinical/field experiences, lab sessions, makeup policy, and similar expectations. Required components are always visible and cannot be hidden.

Required in the CSU template:

  • Instructor Information
  • Required Texts, Materials, or Software
  • Course Learning Objectives/Learning Outcomes
  • Course Requirements and/or Course Policies
  • Evaluation Criteria for Course
  • Calendar/Schedule

Sections That Don't Apply to Your Course

If a non-required section does not apply to your course, leave it blank. Blank sections will not appear on the published version of your syllabus that students see. Required sections cannot be left blank.

5. Course Artificial Intelligence (AI) Policy: Choose One Option

The CSU Baseline Statement is the default. You may keep it or choose one of four options from the dropdown in the component. Pick the option that matches what your assignments ask students to do, and keep all AI language in this component only, so your syllabus never gives two different answers.

  • Default, CSU Baseline: AI use is permitted only with explicit authorization from the instructor. Unauthorized use is treated as academic misconduct under the CSU Student Code of Conduct.
  • Option 1, GenAI Use Permitted with Disclosure: Students may use GenAI if all use is disclosed: the tool, the purpose, and how the output was reviewed, revised, or integrated.
  • Option 2, GenAI Use Permitted for Specific Activities Only: Only the listed uses are allowed. The permitted and prohibited lists are examples; customize them for your course.
  • Option 3, GenAI Use Encouraged (With Attribution): Students are encouraged to use GenAI, must cite or acknowledge it, and remain accountable for accuracy and originality.
  • Option 4, GenAI Use Prohibited: GenAI is not permitted for any assignment or activity unless the instructor explicitly authorizes it.

6. Protect Your Work: Visible/Hidden and Public/Private

Each component you can edit has two toggles under its name:

Visible / Hidden

Controls whether a component appears on your syllabus. Required components are locked to Visible (a lock icon displays next to the slider).

Public / Private: your intellectual property protection

Private components are visible only to students enrolled in the section, the section's instructors, and Simple Syllabus administrators. Public components are visible to anyone who can view the syllabus, which at CSU means the campus community through single sign-on.

Why Private matters: when another instructor imports from your syllabus, your Private components are not included. Only Public components can be imported by someone else. Set original work, such as detailed assignment designs, rubrics, or teaching materials, to Private.

Add, Reorder, and Import

Add: Click Add, name your new component, and it is added to the bottom of your syllabus.

Reorder:

  1. Click Reorder on the left navigation.
  2. In the Reorder components pop-up, drag and drop components into your desired order. The reordering happens as you drag.
  3. Click Close to save the new order.

Replace with a different syllabus: At the top of the syllabus, click Replace with a different syllabus to start from another syllabus you were assigned to. Search by term or keyword, click the syllabus, then update anything specific to the new section and click Submit. Your Private components, including components you added, copy over.

Import: Click Import to bring content from another syllabus or Course Master. Select the term, subject, course number, and section, then click Finalize to import all eligible components, or turn on Advanced to choose which components to copy. Confirm with Start import of the following content.

7. Calendar/Schedule and SmartView Fields

Brightspace calendar sync is not turned on. Build your weekly schedule in the Calendar/Schedule component with + Add new item. Write important labels such as "Exam week" in words; do not rely on color.

SmartView fields let a schedule link each row to course objectives or instructors, like the example below. They are not part of the Calendar/Schedule component in the CSU template. If you would like a schedule with SmartView fields, email elearning@csu.edu for help setting it up, or read SmartView Fields (opens in new tab).

Attaching Documents

Attachments are turned off so syllabi stay readable on any device and pass the accessibility check. If your course truly needs a file, email elearning@csu.edu. Examples of when an attachment may make sense:

  • A form students must download, complete, and sign, such as a lab safety agreement or field placement agreement
  • A handbook required by an accreditor or program, such as a clinical or practicum handbook
  • A detailed rubric or assignment packet too long for a syllabus component

Any attachment must be accessible. A grading table, schedule, or policy should be built in the syllabus itself, not attached.

8. Course Masters (Multi-Section Courses)

A Course Master is the course-level document that affects all sections of a course. For example, if ENG-1101 has sections 01, 02, and 03, submitting the ENG-1101 Course Master sends its content to the syllabi for all three sections.

  • It covers only the components that are editable at the course level on the template.
  • It is edited by a course-level role (called Designer by default).
  • Next term, a Course Master submitted in a past term auto-completes once Course Master editing is turned on; designers can still make changes and resubmit.

Be your department's Course Master champion. There are currently no Course Master editors, and we welcome a faculty champion from each department. To volunteer, or to request a Course Master for your course, email elearning@csu.edu.

9. Submit Your Syllabus

  • All changes save automatically. If you close the syllabus before submitting, it stays in your To Do column as In progress.
  • When every required component is complete, click Submit at the bottom. You can add an optional comment, which appears in the syllabus History feed.
  • Can't submit? Click the warning message at the bottom of the syllabus to see which components are missing content. Then run the accessibility checker; it must pass before you can submit.
  • Approvals are not enabled, so your syllabus moves to the Completed column and publishes when you submit.

10. After You Submit

View, Print, and Share: Under the Completed column, click View for the web version, Print to download a PDF or print, and Share to copy the syllabus URL or PDF URL.

If a student sees an error, check the When Students See an Error table on the Support tab.

Students read 80%, then acknowledge: Students have a minimum read percentage of 80 and must acknowledge the syllabus. Simple Syllabus tracks how far each student has gone through the syllabus, measured toward the final component. Acknowledgment shows a student got through the syllabus, not that they understood it. If understanding matters, pair it with a short syllabus quiz or a first-week discussion in Brightspace.

Track Student Engagement

In Brightspace:

  1. Open your course and select My Materials, then My Syllabus.
  2. The horizontal engagement bar shows how many students have viewed the syllabus compared with those who have not.
  3. Click the bar or View Report for a breakdown by student.

In Simple Syllabus: In the Completed column of your dashboard, click the horizontal bar under the syllabus.

The report lists each enrolled student, how many times they viewed the syllabus, and the last viewed date. Export it as a CSV to also see percent read, acknowledgment status, and acknowledgment timestamp:

Edit a Completed Syllabus

  • During the term: Click Edit in My Syllabus in Brightspace, or under the syllabus in your Completed column. Make your changes (they save automatically), then click Submit again.
  • After the term ends: Syllabi can be edited through the last day of the term (the Banner term end date). After that they are archived, and the edit icon is grayed out.

Cross-Listed Sections

If you teach cross-listed sections, clicking My Syllabus in Brightspace shows an error message. This happens because a cross-listed section is coded differently from a single section, and Brightspace cannot show several syllabi in one view. The error page lists links to the syllabus for each section you are enrolled in, so you can choose the one to edit or view. Students see the same choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Please ensure third-party cookies are not blocked. To unblock in Google Chrome:

  1. Open Chrome
  2. Click More (three dots) then Settings in the top right
  3. Click Privacy and Security
  4. Click Third-party cookies
  5. Select Allow third-party cookies

On iPad, iPhone, and mobile devices: Ensure "Allow Cross-Website Tracking" is enabled.

Safari: Ensure "Prevent Cross-Site Tracking" is disabled.

Pulse, Chrome, and other 3rd-party apps: Ensure "Allow Cross-Site Tracking" is enabled.
