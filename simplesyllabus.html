How Do I Access and Edit My Syllabus?

You can access your syllabus in two ways:

  • From Brightspace: Go to brightspace.csu.edu (opens in new tab), click into your course, select the My Materials tab, then click My Syllabus to begin editing.
  • From Simple Syllabus directly: Go to chicagostate.simplesyllabus.com (opens in new tab) and log in with your CSU Single Sign-On (SSO). Click the edit icon on your syllabus to begin.

Instructor View in Simple Syllabus

Home Page: Contains your syllabi organized as To Do and Completed items.

Syllabus Library Tab: Filter by term to view previously published syllabi. The View, Print, and Share icons appear when you hover over a syllabus.

General Editing Tips

  • Your most recent content will automatically import when editing a syllabus if you have previously submitted the same course.
  • After the first set of syllabi are published for Fall 2026, you will see the option to Import ("Would you like to import a different syllabus?"), which lets you pull from any syllabus you have published in the past.
  • All changes are automatically saved while editing. Your syllabus will not be live until the publishing process is initiated.
  • Help text is included on the right side of the syllabus to guide you through editing each component.
  • For best results, type new content rather than pasting. Use the built-in editor to set font, size, color, style, and spacing. Pasting can cause unpredictable formatting.

Required Components (Orange Outline)

Components with an orange border and a "Required" label at the bottom must be completed before you can submit. For example, Course Requirements and/or Course Policies is a required component where you add your attendance policy, class participation, exams, quizzes, papers, technology assignments, clinical/field experiences, lab sessions, makeup policy, etc.

Each required component includes a rich text editor with formatting tools and a help text panel on the right side with guidance on what to include. You can also toggle whether a section is Visible or Public.

Sections That Don't Apply to Your Course

If a non-required section does not apply to your course, simply leave it blank. Blank sections will not appear on the published version of your syllabus that students see.

Understanding Visible/Hidden vs. Public/Private

Each component has two toggle sliders that control who can see it:

Visible / Hidden

Controls whether a component appears in your syllabus at all. If a component is required, this toggle is disabled and locked to Visible (a lock icon will display next to the slider).

Public / Private

Protects your intellectual property. Public components are viewable by anyone in the Syllabus Library. Private components are only accessible to enrolled students, the instructor, and administrators.

Locked Sections (Institutional Content)

Some sections are locked and cannot be edited by instructors. These contain campus-wide policies managed at the institutional level, such as Campus Policies and the Statement from Abilities Office. This ensures all syllabi include consistent, university-approved language.

If you notice an error in a locked section, contact CTRE/OOIT so it can be corrected at the template level.

Printing Your Document / Downloading PDF

Once your document is complete, you will be able to View, Print, and Share:

  • View: Click the View button to see a digital version of your syllabus.
  • Download or Print: Click the Print button to download a PDF or send it to a printer.
  • Share: Click the Share button to copy the syllabus URL or PDF URL.

Frequently Asked Questions

Your account information (email, ID number, etc.) may not be correct in Simple Syllabus, or your enrollment may have had an issue during the file import. Contact support@simplesyllabus.com so they can verify the email address on file and reset your password if needed.

Review your syllabus and ensure all required fields have been completed. Required components will be outlined in orange with "Required" visible at the bottom. All required components must have content before the submit button becomes active.

Please ensure third-party cookies are not blocked. To unblock in Google Chrome:

  1. Open Chrome
  2. Click More (three dots) then Settings in the top right
  3. Click Privacy and Security
  4. Click Third-party cookies
  5. Select Allow third-party cookies

On iPad, iPhone, and mobile devices: Ensure "Allow Cross-Website Tracking" is enabled.

Safari: Ensure "Prevent Cross-Site Tracking" is disabled.

Pulse, Chrome, and other 3rd-party apps: Ensure "Allow Cross-Site Tracking" is enabled.
